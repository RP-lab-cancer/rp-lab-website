New preprint on stochprofIC is here: a method that separates noise and batch effects from real signal in mini-bulk omics, letting us recover the single-cell populations hidden within each sample, including a MAP3K11-high state tied to apoptosis in breast-cancer organoids.
[Check the manuscript here.](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.09.765210v1)
