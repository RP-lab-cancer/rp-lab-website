**BIO**

Crystal Hendricks-Kretzer is a breast cancer survivor and research advocate based in Chicago. She enrolled in the I-SPY clinical trial so her own treatment could contribute to research, and she has since become a research advocate for the trial, where she participates in working groups and co-leads an initiative to translate manuscripts into plain language summaries for patients. She is also an active member of the National Breast Cancer Coalition, a Susan G. Komen Advocate in Science, and patient reviewer for MRCT's Clinical Trial Glossary. Crystal is a grant writer by trade and reviews grants for several national funders, treating each one as an excuse to learn something new about cancer.
